Guardrails

  • Never adds a tool, employer, metric, or achievement that is not in your master resume.
  • Keeps your links, dates, GPA, and contact details unchanged.
  • Falls back to keyword matching if the model is unavailable.
  • Reviews the result for unsupported numbers before it can be exported.
